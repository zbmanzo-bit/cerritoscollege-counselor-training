📌 Executive Summary

The 2026 UC Ensuring Transfer Success (ETS) conference highlighted record-breaking transfer application volumes across the University of California system alongside major structural shifts in transfer pathways. The system-wide UC Dual Admission pilot is being sunset effective January 2027 due to low yield, redirecting system focus toward Associate Degree for Transfer (ADT) expansion across UC campuses. UC Merced launched a comprehensive ADT initiative featuring a $4,000 annual scholarship, while UCLA completed its initial ADT pilot with a remarkable 65% admit rate. To manage severe capacity constraints—where over 50% of applicants concentrate on fewer than 10 majors—campuses like UC Irvine are removing impacted majors (STEM, Business, Psychology) from TAG starting Fall 2028. Concurrently, campuses are strictly enforcing pre-transfer major preparation completion (UC Davis now rescinds offers for missing single prep courses) and cross-checking dual enrollment records via the National Student Clearinghouse to eliminate unreported high school college units.

🌐 Cross-Campus Themes

Common strategic initiatives and policy patterns identified across multiple UC campuses during ETS 2026.

  • 1. ADT Pathway Expansion UC Merced, UCLA, UC Riverside, UC Santa Barbara, and UC Santa Cruz are actively building or expanding ADT-guaranteed pathways to streamline junior-level graduation.
  • 2. Major Concentration & TAG Impaction Extreme concentration in top majors (CS, Business, Psychology, Bio) is forcing TAG eliminations (UCI Fall 2028) and strict major prep enforcement (UC Davis, UCSB).
  • 3. Dual Enrollment Audits Campuses (UCLA, UCR, UCOP) are actively auditing applicants against the National Student Clearinghouse to catch unlisted college courses taken during high school.
  • 4. Multi-Year Housing Guarantees 2-Year transfer housing guarantees are now established at UCSC, UCSB, UCD, and UCR, backed by significant new residential developments.

🦅 Cerritos College Actionable Intelligence Hub

Translating UC ETS 2026 system trends into specific, actionable local strategies for Cerritos College leadership, counseling, articulation, and student services departments.

Idea #1 • Source: UC Merced Priority: High

1. UC Merced ADT Guarantee & Scholarship Alignment

What They Are Doing: UC Merced launched a full ADT guarantee offering guaranteed admission, a $4,000/year renewable scholarship ($8,000 total), and a 60-unit completion cap post-transfer for ADTs in Biology, Anthropology, Liberal Studies, History, Political Science, and Social Justice.

Problem Addressed: High transfer costs and excess unit accumulation beyond 60 units post-transfer.
Cerritos Relevance: Directly benefits Cerritos ADT earners seeking a clear, guaranteed, highly affordable UC pathway.
Departments Involved: Transfer Center, Counseling, Articulation Officer Suggested Next Step: Map Cerritos existing ADTs in Biology, History, PoliSci, & Social Justice to UCM's ADT list.
Idea #2 • Source: UCLA / UCR / UCOP Priority: Critical Compliance

2. Dual Enrollment Auditing & Advising Disclosure Protocols

What They Are Doing: Checking application data against National Student Clearinghouse records and factoring all dual enrollment GPAs directly into transfer evaluation.

Problem Addressed: Rescinded transfer offers due to unlisted high school college courses or repeated C-grade coursework.
Cerritos Relevance: Cerritos high school dual enrollment students need clear warnings that college courses taken during high school permanently impact UC transfer GPAs.
Departments Involved: Dual Enrollment Office, High School Outreach, Counseling, Admissions & Records Suggested Next Step: Create a mandatory "Dual Enrollment to UC Transfer" disclosure form for dual enrollment students.
Idea #3 • Source: UC Irvine & UC Riverside Priority: Medium

3. Interactive Major Exploration & Alternative Pathway Mapping

What They Are Doing: Utilizing online interactive major quizzes and career-centered major mapping tools to direct students away from hyper-impacted majors toward non-impacted alternatives.

Problem Addressed: Over 50% of transfer applicants concentrate on under 10 hyper-competitive majors, causing high rejection rates.
Cerritos Relevance: Helps Cerritos counselors guide students to alternative majors with higher admit rates within the same discipline.
Departments Involved: Counseling, Career Services, Transfer Center Suggested Next Step: Embed UCI's Major Quiz and UCR's Career-Major Map links into Cerritos Transfer Center advising workshops.

💬 Counseling & Student Services

Advising workflows must adapt immediately to UCI’s Fall 2028 TAG removals and UC Davis’s strict zero-missing-course major prep policy. Students attempting 1-year transfers must be strongly cautioned against extreme credit overloading in spring terms to avoid severe burnout or rescinded admissions.

🏫 Dual Enrollment & High School Outreach

High school students taking college courses must be explicitly advised that grades earned follow them on permanent UC transfer transcripts and affect major prep GPAs. Coursework with C grades or higher cannot be repeated to raise GPAs.

🔬 STEM & SEM Division

UCSB is adding Actuarial Science and Data Science to selective major reviews (Fall 2027). The system-wide Biological Sciences UCP consolidation (combining 4 bio tracks into 1) requires updated local degree audit tracking maps.

🎯 Transfer Center & Career Pathways

Actively promote non-impacted major options and alternative degree pathways using UCI and UCR major mapping tools during workshops to raise overall Cerritos student UC admission rates.
